## Read an observation

An exact envelope from line 16:

```json
{"path":"eval/observation/provider-effect-activity","msg":["DATA",{"kind":"eval-effect-class-activity-snapshot","effectClass":"provider","activeEffects":0,"activeExecutionIds":[],"admittedEffects":0,"settledEffects":0}],"tier":3,"seq":17}
```

`path` names the actual producing node. `msg` is its protocol message; only DATA contains a business value. `seq` orders observer delivery, not business identity. The canonical occurrence stream is `eval/observation/canonical-state`: line 2136 contains occurrence revision 157, the terminal coherent cut. Read its `occurrenceId/revision/digest/sourceRefs` for correlation and its `value` for progress, active effects, budget, finding and stopping reason. `eval/observation` is the value projection of that same producer.

Transport contributions at `eval/observation/arrivals` retain per-source revisions. An exact older replay is quiet; a contradictory or skipped revision produces `eval/observation/rejections`. Observation rejection does not mutate admission/budget authority. A full observation is published only when its domain coordinates form a coherent cut; it is not promised for every individual internal contribution.

## Boundaries and remaining risks

The HTTP transport runtime is installed by the live-shaped executor into the same supplied Graph. The standard describe artifact above captures the core campaign topology with the scripted executor; HTTP-leaf topology/privacy is separately exercised by `root-eval-http-transport-leaf.test.ts`.

Join controllers have bounded graph/bundle-lifetime retention, not per-subscriber lifetime. Unsubscribing one public output does not reset its occurrence ledger. This implementation does not claim dynamic topology-group removal of those retained bundles.

An exploratory probe containing more than 13,184 repeated full diagnostic envelopes hit a 120-second CPU timeout (~849 MB worker RSS). It is **not a passing benchmark**. The actual 175-call campaign conservation test passes; pathological log-processing cost remains a diagnostic-profile/benchmark concern. The evidence bound is not a promise that processing every maximum-sized diagnostic trace is inexpensive.

No credential, browser, provider or live spend was used for this qualification. Earlier live generations and charges remain historical; the consumed D152 development-1 claim cannot be reused.
